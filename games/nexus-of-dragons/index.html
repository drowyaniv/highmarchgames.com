Nexus of Dragons

In developmentPC (Windows)1–6 players

A fantasy adventure board game for one to six players. Cross Thandrys, fight and grow stronger on every road, and be the one who defeats the dragon and claims the Crystal.

Screenshots

The game

A board game you can lose yourself in

Nexus of Dragons is played on a board of three rings, across the land of Thandrys. Each turn you roll, choose your road and face what you find there: a monster to fight, a stranger with an offer, a town to rest in, a gate that will only open for the strong.

Your hero grows with every step – levels, spells, gear, a horse, a few loyal companions. The other heroes on the board are racing you to the same prize. Only one of you will take mastery of the Nexus Crystal.

To win, reach the Nexus Temple, defeat the dragon that guards it and take mastery of the Crystal.

Features

What waits on the road

  • Journey to the heart

    Three rings of land – the Outer, the Middle and the Inner – each harder than the last. Find the crossings, earn your way inward and reach the Nexus Temple.

  • Heroes to choose from

    Dozens of ready-made heroes from many races and callings, each with a story and a personal quest of their own. Or make your own hero.

  • Dice on the table

    Every blow, spell and saving throw is rolled out in front of you, from the d4 to the d20. Classic tabletop rules decide every fight.

  • Towns and the road

    Rest at inns, trade in markets, buy a mount at the stables, hire companions in taverns, take guild contracts – and perhaps raise a dragon of your own.

  • The lich's lieutenants

    As the heroes grow stronger, four of the lich's servants rise and walk the land. Beat them for their treasures – or run when you must.

  • Your table, your length

    Up to six players in any mix of friends at one screen and computer heroes. Play a Classic match for the long road or a Quick one for an evening.

The Waking of the Nexus

Now the dead march again

For eight hundred years the Crystal has burned half-open, corrupted by the souls the lich Vael Morrath poured into it. Every few decades it flares: the monsters come, the dead walk, and its guardian rises to burn the land. Now the dead march again, and someone is leading them. Whoever finishes the ritual will hold the Nexus.

Heroes

Dozens of heroes, each with a story

A gate captain searching for the man who raised him. A library guard who has kept her lady's last order for eight hundred years. A hunter tracking a corrupted dragon through the marshes. Choose one of them – or make your own.
