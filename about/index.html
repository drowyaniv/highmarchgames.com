About

Highmarch Games

A one-person independent game studio, making games worth coming back to.

I care about rules that are easy to learn and hard to master, worlds with a history behind them, and heroes who are more than a set of numbers. My first game, Nexus of Dragons, is a fantasy adventure board game for one to six players, in development for PC. I make my games with the help of AI tools – for the artwork, the music, some of the sound and the game text – and I direct, review and rework every piece until it fits.
