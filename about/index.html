About

Highmarch Games

An independent studio making fantasy games – the kind you sit down to for an evening and look up from hours later.

We care about worlds that feel painted rather than generated in bulk, rules that are easy to learn and hard to master, and heroes who are more than a set of numbers. Our first game, Nexus of Dragons, is a fantasy adventure board game for one to six players, in development for PC.
