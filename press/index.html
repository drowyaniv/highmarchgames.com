Description

Short: A fantasy adventure board game for one to six players. Choose a hero, journey inward across three rings of a painted land, and face the Shadow Dragon at the heart of the Nexus.

Long: Nexus of Dragons is a fantasy adventure board game for one to six players, in development for PC. Players choose from ninety heroes - nine peoples, five classes, each with a story and a personal quest - and journey inward across three rings of a painted land. Along the way they fight monsters with dice on the table, rest and trade in towns, buy mounts, hire companions and take on contracts, while four lieutenants of the Shadow Dragon roam the board. The first hero to reach the Nexus Temple, defeat the Shadow Dragon and take mastery of the Nexus Crystal wins. Matches come in two lengths: Classic and Quick.

Features

  • Journey to the heart

    Three rings of land - the Outer, the Middle and the Inner - each harder than the last. Find the crossings, earn your way inward and reach the Nexus Temple.

  • Ninety heroes

    Nine peoples and five callings: warriors, mages, clerics, rogues and rangers, each hero with a story and a personal quest of their own. Or make your own hero.

  • Dice on the table

    Every blow, spell and saving throw is rolled out in front of you, from the d4 to the d20. Classic tabletop rules decide every fight.

  • Towns and the road

    Rest at inns, trade in markets, buy a mount at the stables, hire companions in taverns, take guild contracts - and perhaps raise a dragon of your own.

  • The Shadow Dragon's lieutenants

    Four of the dragon's servants walk the land. Beat them for their treasures - or run when you must.

  • Your table, your length

    Up to six players in any mix of friends at one screen and computer heroes. Play a Classic match for the long road or a Quick one for an evening.
